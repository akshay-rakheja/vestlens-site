Your portfolio, in focus.

VestLens puts your Wealthsimple accounts inside Claude and ChatGPT: read-only, in Canadian dollars, with the numbers Canadian investors actually need. It's open source, and you run your own copy with your own SnapTrade key.

What you can do with it

Ask about your portfolio in chat

Ask Claude or ChatGPT how your accounts are doing. Answers come with interactive charts and tables, not just text.

See returns net of your deposits

What your money actually earned, separate from what you put in, next to the same deposits made into an index fund.

Track TFSA and FHSA room

A year-by-year ledger of your contribution room, move by move, starting from your CRA My Account figure.

Holdings, allocation and income

What you own across accounts, how it's split, and the dividends and interest it pays.

Every buy and sell on one chart

A holding's price with your own trades marked on it, so you can see when you bought and when you sold.

Canadian by default

Amounts in CAD. US-dollar holdings are converted at the Bank of Canada rate and labelled as estimates, with the original amount kept.

How it works

  1. Get a SnapTrade Personal key

    SnapTrade is the service VestLens uses to read your brokerage. A Personal key is free and covers your own accounts only. You connect Wealthsimple on SnapTrade's page, so VestLens never sees your Wealthsimple password.

  2. Run your own copy

    VestLens runs with Docker on your own computer or a small server you control. Your data stays in your copy's own database.

  3. Add it to Claude or ChatGPT

    Give your copy a public HTTPS address and add it as a custom connector. Your copy asks you to sign in before it shows anything.

Read-only, and yours

  • It can't trade or move money. VestLens never calls SnapTrade's trading, order or transfer functions.
  • One person per copy. Your copy reads only the accounts on your own SnapTrade key.
  • Nothing comes back to us. No analytics, no tracking, and no account with the VestLens project.

Who it's for

VestLens is for people comfortable running software themselves. You'll use a terminal, Docker and a settings file, and to use it in Claude or ChatGPT you'll need somewhere to host it. If that isn't you, Wealthsimple has announced its own official ChatGPT and Claude connections, expected in early 2027.
