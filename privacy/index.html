Privacy

Updated November 10, 2026.

The hosted service is closed

The hosted VestLens service closed on November 9, 2026. All user data, brokerage connections, backups and sign-in records were deleted. VestLens no longer holds anyone's information.

This website

  • This site is static. It sets no cookies, has no accounts or forms, and runs no analytics or tracking. Its fonts are served from this site.
  • The TFSA tools work entirely in your browser. What you type isn't sent anywhere. The TFSA by age page remembers the age group you picked in your browser's own storage, and you can clear it by clearing this site's data.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Your own copy of VestLens

If you run VestLens yourself, it runs on hardware you control and keeps its data in your own database. It talks to SnapTrade with your own key, and to optional news services only if you turn them on. It sends nothing to the VestLens project. SnapTrade's privacy policy covers what SnapTrade holds.
