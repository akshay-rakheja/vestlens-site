During 2024 they put in $8,938 over 23.8 deposits and took out $9,548 over 7.6 withdrawals.

People aged 30 to 34 averaged 7.6 withdrawals. Room from a withdrawal comes back on 1 January of the next year, not right away. Putting the money back sooner uses this year's room, and CRA charges 1% a month on any amount over.

The lenses are drawn to scale: the disc is the average account, the ring is the average room left unused. They're averages, not medians, so a few large accounts pull them up; CRA doesn't publish medians for TFSAs.

See your own lens next to these

VestLens is an open-source app you run yourself. With your Wealthsimple accounts connected through your own SnapTrade key, it works out your TFSA year move by move: each deposit, withdrawal and transfer, the room it used, and the day you went over, if you did. It starts from your room on January 1 in CRA My Account. Wealthsimple history before about April 2023 isn't available to it, so check its limitations first.

Read-only: VestLens can't trade or move money. You sign in to your brokerage on SnapTrade's page, never in VestLens.
