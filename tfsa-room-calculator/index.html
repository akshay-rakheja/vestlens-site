How TFSA room works

Your room is this year's limit, plus any room you didn't use in earlier years, plus what you withdrew last year, minus what you've contributed this year. Gains, losses and fees don't change it.

When room starts

It starts the year you turn 18, and that year's full limit counts even if your birthday is in December. It keeps adding up whether or not you've opened a TFSA. In some provinces you have to be 19 to open one, but you keep the room from the year you turned 18. New residents start from the year they became a resident.

When withdrawals come back

On January 1 of the next year, not right away. Putting money back in the same year uses this year's room, and if you don't have enough, it's an over-contribution even if it was a mistake. The exception is a withdrawal made while you're over: it lowers the excess right away, and only the rest comes back on January 1.

Transfers between TFSAs

A direct transfer between your own TFSAs doesn't change your room. If you withdraw the money and deposit it yourself, it counts as a withdrawal and a new contribution.
